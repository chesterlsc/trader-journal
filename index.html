        Firm rules

        These are your numbers, not a rule book. This app tracks the limits you enter here against your own closed trades. It is not a source of truth on any firm’s current rules and it gives no trading advice. Confirm every figure against your own account before you rely on it.

        Prefills only. The figures were read from public documentation on a stated date and are reproduced here to save typing. Firms change them, and during this feature’s research one firm’s own two pages disagreed with each other. Check yours.

        A $0 start is an Express-Funded style account. The tracker uses $0 as this account’s base, which is correct; the dashboard equity curve keeps plotting from the starting balance in Risk Controls, because percentage and position-size maths off a $0 base would be meaningless.

        How the scores are calculated

        Discipline Score
        Starts at 100. Deductions: −10 per risk-per-trade breach, −8 per daily-loss breach, −10 per weekly-loss breach, −5 per Emotional or Revenge trade, −4 per C/F execution grade, −6 per “No” and −3 per “Partially” rules reflection. +2 per A+ execution or Perfect Execution trade. Clamped to 0–100.
        Daily Trading Score
        Scores your most recent trading day from a base of 50: +20 for a profitable day or −10 for a losing one, +15 for staying inside the daily loss limit or −15 for breaching it, −10 per Emotional/Revenge trade, +5 per A/A+ execution, and +15 / +5 / −10 for a Yes / Partially / No rules reflection. Clamped to 0–100.
        Trader Score
        The average of six 0–100 readings: win rate, profit factor (3.0 scores 100), average win/loss ratio (2.5 scores 100), recovery factor (4.0 scores 100), max drawdown as a share of starting balance (12% scores 0, inverted), and consistency (share of profitable trading days).
