1fFeatures I'd add — ranked

Everything below is built on data the app already collects. Nothing here needs a new integration, and the top three are what turn the journal from a record into a system.

01

The unjournalled queue

A trade that closed without a note is an incomplete row, and the app should say so. A counted badge in the dock, a card on the dashboard, and a two-minute sheet per trade. This is the single change that will move your data quality, because the psychology fields are the ones people skip and the ones every report depends on. Pairs with a journal streak so there's a reason to keep it at zero.

02

Pre-trade checklist, attached to the trade

Three or four rules you write once, shown inside the New Trade sheet, stored with the row. Today the Discipline Score is calculated after the fact from psychology tags. With this it becomes causal: "the nine trades where you skipped the news check are −$1,240." Costs one tap and makes an existing number honest.

03

Cooldown lock

The loss budgets already exist and already know when they're breached — right now they only colour a bar. Let them do something: at the daily limit, or after N losses in a row, the Log-a-trade button changes state and asks one question before it unlocks. Not a hard block, a speed bump. Revenge Trade is a tag in your schema; this is the feature that reduces the count.

04

Playbook pages

Each setup gets a page: its expectancy curve, its best session, its screenshots side by side. The Edge Detection table already computes every number — it just has nowhere to go.

05

Equity scrub

Drag along the equity curve and the trades under your finger stream past with their notes and screenshots. Turns the drawdown from a shape into a story.

06

Voice notes

Thirty seconds of talking beats a paragraph nobody types. Transcribe on save so it stays searchable next to the written notes.

07

Sunday digest

A drafted weekly review — your numbers, your worst rule break, your best trade — which you edit rather than write. Feeds the Monthly Review that currently starts as a blank textarea.

Things I would cut

The landing page's fake dashboard mock — the live tape does that job honestly. The six-item top nav, which spends a full row on destinations you visit weekly. And "Advanced Details": burying psychology behind a disclosure is what guarantees it stays empty.
